# What Is Cognitive Neuroscience?

Study notes for the first lecture of the **Cognition and Neuroscience** course (A.Y. 2025/2026), University of Bologna.

## Contents

- `WhatIsCognitiveNeuroscience.md` — Full structured study notes with TA explanations, exam-relevant callouts, and case study breakdowns.

## Topics Covered

- Definitions: neuroscience, cognition, cognitive neuroscience
- Brain as a model for AI (arguments for and against)
- Levels of brain emulation in AI (Blue Brain vs. DeepMind)
- Brain structure and function: the 6 subdivisions
- Split-brain and hemispheric specialization
- Language areas: Broca's & Wernicke's aphasia
- Memory systems: Patient H.M. & the hippocampus
- Neural plasticity & Hebb's rule
- Case studies: Phineas Gage (vmPFC), hemispatial neglect
